## Decision 1 — how do we reach the 20-puzzle floor, given promotion alone cannot?
Answer: Hide below-floor categories — promote whatever promotion can reach, hide every category still below the floor at launch, and log fresh candidate generation as a **post-launch `OI-N`**. This launches with roughly 4–6 categories instead of 12, which is fastest and consistent with the epic's "removes blockers, does not add features" rule. The per-environment measurement from Blocking issue 1 must be taken **first** — production queues may be healthier than local and could move several categories into reach for free. This decision **supersedes D2 as written**: D2 must be rewritten from "`forced_win` only" to "any category still short after promotion," and its answer is already fixed to "hide, do not generate."

## Decision 2 — how wide is the stale-answer-data audit?
Answer: Audit all three, repair on finding — run the read-only re-derivation audit across all three metadata-graded categories (`overcut`, `undercut`, `z_threat` — all three read `puzzle.highlightMetadata` in `connect-four/server/predicates.js`), and repair only where drift is actually found. The audit is cheap and read-only, so only a positive finding expands scope. Drift found outside `overcut` becomes a new `OI-N` and a launch-gate conversation, not silent scope creep. SC-002's guarantee stays as written, since the audit now covers the surface it claims.

## Deferrals
- **Fresh candidate generation for below-floor puzzle categories** — deferred to post-launch as a new `OI-N`. At launch, categories that cannot reach the N=20 floor by promotion alone are hidden from serving rather than generated. Rationale: generation costs solver compute and wall-clock across up to six categories and would be the single largest launch delay; hiding is consistent with the epic's "removes blockers, does not add features" rule.
