## Decision 2 — What environment does `tests/` get in the split ESLint config?
Answer: Three environments — browser globals for `src/`, node globals for `server/` + `scripts/`, and node PLUS browser globals for `tests/`. Record this in the spec as a CLARIFICATION of Settled decision #1, not a contradiction: 8 files under `connect-four/tests/` are the jsdom DOM suite and genuinely use `document`, `window`, `localStorage`, so a pure-node ruleset there manufactures new `no-undef` errors that "lint exits 0" would then force someone to suppress — trading real errors for suppressions is the exact failure mode OI-70 exists to fix. Settled decision #1's stated intent ("that alone should clear most of the 254") is better served by three environments.
