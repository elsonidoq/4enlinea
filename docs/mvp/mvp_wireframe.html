Why it works
Two threats, one turn
When you create two winning threats at the same time, your opponent faces an impossible choice. They only get one move — and one move can only block one threat. The other one wins.
💡 The key is timing: both threats must become active on the same turn. Setting up one threat at a time lets your opponent respond to each one.
