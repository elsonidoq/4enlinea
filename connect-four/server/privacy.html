Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Who we are

DecodeC4 is operated by Pablo Zivic, a sole proprietor based in Argentina, trading as DecodeC4. "We" and "us" below refer to that sole proprietorship.

What we collect

  • Email address — collected via Google OAuth when you sign in. Used only to identify your account.
  • Puzzle attempt data — which puzzles you tried, outcomes, and timing. Used to track your progress.
  • Game and position data — the games you play or import, and the board positions you ask us to analyse.
  • Subscription status — whether you have an active paid plan, so we can unlock Pro features.

We do not collect or store your card number or payment details. Payments are handled entirely by Paddle (see below).

How we use your data

Your data is used solely to power your learning progress within DecodeC4, and to provide and bill the service. We do not show ads, and we do not sell your data. We share it with third parties only as needed to operate the service, as listed below.

Third-party services

  • Paddle — our online reseller and Merchant of Record. Paddle handles checkout, payment processing, billing, and refunds, and collects the payment and billing information you enter at checkout. Subject to Paddle's Privacy Policy.
  • Google OAuth — for sign-in. Subject to Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Supabase — for authentication and data storage. Data is stored in the EU/US per Supabase's infrastructure.
  • OpenAI and Anthropic — when you request a detailed explanation of a position (a "Deep Reveal"), the board position and related game context are sent to these providers to generate the explanation. Your email address and account identifiers are not sent. Subject to OpenAI's Privacy Policy and Anthropic's Privacy Policy.

Data retention

Your account and progress data are retained while your account is active. You may request deletion by contacting us. Records relating to payments are retained by Paddle as Merchant of Record for as long as their own legal and tax obligations require, independently of your DecodeC4 account.

Your rights

You may request a copy of your data, correction of inaccurate data, or deletion of your account, by emailing us. For payment records held by Paddle, you may also contact Paddle directly.
